# Contributing

Share an observed problem before proposing more process. Include the intended task, relevant constraints, what happened, and the smallest useful reproduction. Distinguish direct observation from interpretation or simulation. Remove personal data, credentials, and private project material before posting.

For a proposed change, explain the expected user or operational benefit, what it costs, and what safeguards or behavior might be lost. Removal and simplification are welcome. A no-change conclusion is valid when evidence is insufficient or the proposed improvement is not worth its cost.

Use meaningful checks and honest limitations rather than a target number of tests or rules. Keep skills independently usable and preserve authorization boundaries.
